Who We Are

The main aim of the organization is to provide support in Education, Environment, Rural Livelihoods, and Women Empowerment. We work for the Holistic Development of Society through empowering the Youth, regenerating our Natural Resources, and promoting Self-Reliance by encouraging Local for Vocal and stimulating Women groups with Skill-based Training Programs.

Meet Our Leadership Board

Our Vision

  • Promote adaptive behaviour and life skills among children and adolescents using an Interdisciplinary approach.
  • Train social work students by providing practice learning-opportunities and establishing community linkages.
  • Design integrated intervention programmes addressing multidimensional needs for sustainable Development.
  • Create a society where Women groups are actively involved in Social Development.

Our Mission

To strive towards Sustainable Development of Regions across parts of Andhra Pradesh and India among children and adolescents, promoting positive Social Infrastructure and fostering the growth of the Social Demographic Dividend.

Our Goals

To guide people in their life choices and options so that they are able to make right decisions according to their aptitude and capabilities.

Celebrating Banjara & Tribal Heritage

A core pillar of our mission is Culture Conservation. We are deeply committed to promoting the culture of tribes and their ethnicity to protect the lives of vulnerable sections of society.

The vibrant Banjara culture, with its rich embroidery, indigenous arts, and traditional lifestyles, represents the soul of our rural heritage. By integrating tribal communities into our Local for Vocal initiatives, we ensure their traditional skills translate into sustainable livelihoods, preserving their identity while fostering economic independence.
